  • Confined space attendants & fire watch personnel

  • Long-term project safety management

Safety Audits & Inspections
Identify gaps before they become incidents. Thorough site audits and compliance inspections.
YSG conducts comprehensive field audits and inspections designed to close the gap between your written safety program and actual field execution — before regulators or incidents do it for you.
  • Site safety audits and walkthroughs
  • OSHA compliance inspections
  • Hazard identification and risk ranking
  • Written audit reports with corrective actions
  • Follow-up verification and trend analysis

OSHA Compliance & Investigation Support
Expert support through OSHA inspections, citations, and corrective action planning.
When regulatory challenges arise, you need an experienced partner by your side — not after the fact. YSG provides knowledgeable support through every phase of the OSHA process.
  • OSHA inspection and investigation support

  • Emergency call-out and rapid OSHA response
  • VPP readiness and evaluation support

Risk Assessment & Hazard Analysis
JHA, PTP, and HRA planning for your most critical and high-risk activities.
Proactive hazard analysis is the foundation of a functional safety program. YSG helps your team identify, evaluate, and control risk before work begins — not after.
  • Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) development
  • Pre-Task Planning (PTP) programs
  • High-Risk Activity (HRA) planning
  • Fall protection plans and programs
  • Lockout/Tagout (LOTO), confined space, excavation
  • Crane, rigging, and lift planning support

Why Yates Safety Group

Built from the Ground Up,
for the People Who Do the Work

Yates Safety Group was founded by Dawn Yates — a safety professional who came up through the skilled trades. With 22 years of field experience, Dawn has worked alongside ironworkers, welders, operators, and laborers. She has seen firsthand what happens when safety is treated as a compliance checkbox — and what's possible when it isn't.

That experience is the foundation of everything YSG does. Not safety from the office, but safety from the field — practical, real-world, and built around the realities your workforce faces every day.

YSG's mission is straightforward: to become a trusted safety partner that helps organizations protect their workforce, reduce risk, strengthen operational performance, and build safety systems that actually work in the field — not just on paper.

At YSG, we believe effective safety management must be practical, proactive, and collaborative. Our commitment is not to help you meet the minimum. Our commitment is to Your People — the ones showing up at 6 AM, doing the real work, and counting on someone to have their back.

Our Promise to Your People

Every worker deserves to go home the same way they arrived. YSG keeps that promise at the center of every audit, every plan, and every coaching conversation.

Field-First Approach

We close the gap between written safety programs and real field execution. Policies matter — but only if they reflect how work actually gets done.

Practical. Proactive. Collaborative.

We don't show up to write reports and leave. YSG works alongside your leadership and crews to build safety that sticks — through accountability, engagement, and trust.

Women-Owned, Industry-Recognized

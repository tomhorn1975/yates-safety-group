Why Yates Safety Group

Built from the trades.
Built for the field.

Yates Safety Group was founded by Theadesa "Dawn" Yates — a safety professional who didn't start behind a desk. Dawn spent 22 years in the skilled trades, working alongside ironworkers, welders, pipefitters, and laborers. She's been in the hard hat at 6 AM. She's been the person who needed the safety plan — and didn't have one.

That experience is the foundation of everything YSG does. When Dawn walks a job site, she isn't reading it from a checklist. She sees it through the eyes of every worker who has ever shown up to do the real work — and she knows exactly what's at stake when safety falls short.

YSG's mission is to become your most trusted safety partner — protecting your workforce, reducing risk, and building systems that actually work, not just on paper. That promise extends beyond the company to the people it serves: Your People.

"Our commitment isn't to help you meet the minimum. It's to the people showing up at 6 AM, doing the real work, counting on someone to have their back. That someone is us."
— Theadesa "Dawn" Yates, Founder & Principal Safety Consultant, YSG

The YSG Worker Document Portal

Imagine a 6 AM project orientation. The PM forgot to request documents from HR, who doesn't arrive until 8. You're paying a journeyman welder to go home for the day — because the paperwork wasn't ready.

YSG is building the solution. A secure, cloud-based document management system where training certifications, drug screen results, and safety records live in one place — instantly accessible to the right people, at the right time.

How It Works

Each worker's hard hat carries a QR code sticker. When they arrive on site, the General Contractor scans it and immediately sees their current training certifications and most recent drug screen result — no calls to HR, no delays, no excuses. Organizations you hire YSG to manage get secure, permission-based access to their folder.

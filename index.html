Risk Assessment & Hazard Analysis

Proactive hazard analysis is the foundation of a functional safety program. YSG helps your team identify, evaluate, and control risk before work begins — not after someone gets hurt.

  • Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) development
  • Pre-Task Planning (PTP) programs
  • High-Risk Activity (HRA) planning
  • Fall protection plans and programs
  • Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) and confined space
  • Crane, rigging, and excavation safety

Why Yates Safety Group

Built from the trades.
Built for the field.

Yates Safety Group was founded by Dawn Yates — a safety professional who came up through the skilled trades. With 22 years of field experience, Dawn has worked alongside ironworkers, welders, operators, and laborers. She has seen firsthand what happens when safety is treated as a compliance checkbox — and what's possible when it isn't.

That experience is the foundation of everything YSG does. Not safety from behind a desk — safety from the field. Practical, real-world, and built around the realities your workforce faces every single day.

YSG's mission is to become a trusted safety partner that helps organizations protect their workforce, reduce risk, strengthen operational performance, and build safety systems that actually work — not just look good on paper.

"Our commitment is not to help you meet the minimum. Our commitment is to Your People — the ones showing up at 6 AM, doing the real work, and counting on someone to have their back."
— Dawn Yates, Founder, Yates Safety Group
